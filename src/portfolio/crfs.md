Case Report Forms (CRFs) are the backbone of clinical trials, used to collect data from patients. I designed and built a library of 50+ modular, reusable and CDISC-compliant CRFs for clinical trials, which significantly reduced the time and effort required to design and build CRFs for clinical trials and improved the accuracy of the data. Allowing us to design new studies in a matter of hours rather than weeks.
